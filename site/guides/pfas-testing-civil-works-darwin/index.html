PFAS testing for civil works around Darwin: what contractors need to know

By Chris Bell, Principal, Bell Enviro · Updated 2026-11-05

PFAS (per- and polyfluoroalkyl substances) are the firefighting foam chemicals found in soil and groundwater around airports, Defence bases and fire training grounds. Around Darwin, investigation areas have been declared around RAAF Base Darwin and Darwin International Airport and extend into surrounding suburbs and drainage lines. Legacy industrial sites and landfills can also be affected.

When excavated material needs PFAS testing

  • Works inside or downstream of a declared PFAS investigation or management area.
  • Material is leaving site for reuse or disposal and the receiving facility asks for PFAS results (increasingly standard).
  • Dewatering will discharge groundwater to stormwater or land.
  • The contract or CEMP requires it.

What the PFAS NEMP means on site

The PFAS National Environmental Management Plan sets the criteria for reuse, management and disposal of PFAS-impacted soil and water. In practice for Top End contractors this means: test before you move material; prefer on-site reuse under a management plan because licensed disposal options for PFAS waste in the NT are limited; and manage dewatering so that impacted groundwater is not spread.

Sampling matters

PFAS is present in waterproof clothing, sunscreen, some sample containers and many field items. Sampling must use PFAS-free equipment and protocols or the results are meaningless. Use a consultant who runs NEMP-compliant protocols and NATA-accredited laboratories.
