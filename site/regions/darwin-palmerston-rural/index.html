Environmental consultant for Darwin, Palmerston and the rural area

This is Bell Enviro's home ground. Being based in the rural area means same-day site attendance across Darwin, Palmerston and Litchfield, no travel charges within the greater Darwin region, and first-hand knowledge of local conditions: Wet-season drainage, acid sulfate soils around the harbour and lowlands, PFAS investigation areas near the airport and base, and the legacy industrial land at East Arm, Berrimah and Winnellie.

Areas covered

Darwin city and northern suburbs, East Arm and Berrimah industrial precincts, Darwin International Airport and RAAF Base precincts, Palmerston and its new suburbs, Humpty Doo, Howard Springs, Coolalinga, Berry Springs, Noonamah, Virginia, Bees Creek, Darwin River and the Litchfield municipality, Cox Peninsula and Dundee.

Typical work in this region

  • Subdivision and roads earthworks CEMPs and ESCPs in Palmerston and Litchfield growth areas
  • PSIs and DSIs for industrial land transactions at East Arm, Berrimah and Winnellie
  • Waste classification and PFAS screening for airport precinct and Defence-adjacent works
  • Quarry and extractive compliance in the Darwin rural area
  • Weed hygiene declarations and clearance for rural area civil works

Services available

  • Contaminated land assessment — PSI and DSI
  • PFAS investigation and management
  • Construction Environmental Management Plans (CEMPs) and ESCPs
  • Environmental auditing, inspections and compliance
  • Environmental approvals and statutory compliance
  • Your environmental department, as a service
  • Cultural heritage assessment and AAPA clearance coordination
  • Compliance, stockpile and incident registers
