Darwin, Palmerston and the rural area

This is home. Bell Enviro is based in Humpty Doo, so site attendance across Darwin, Palmerston and Litchfield is same-day and there's no travel charge within the greater Darwin area. It also means local knowledge that doesn't come from a desktop: Wet-season drainage, acid sulfate soils around the harbour and the lowlands, the PFAS areas near the airport and base, and the legacy industrial land at East Arm, Berrimah and Winnellie.

Areas covered

Darwin city and the northern suburbs, East Arm and Berrimah, the airport and RAAF base precincts, Palmerston and its new suburbs, Humpty Doo, Howard Springs, Coolalinga, Berry Springs, Noonamah, Virginia, Bees Creek, Darwin River and the rest of Litchfield, Cox Peninsula and Dundee.

Typical work in this region

  • CEMPs and ESCPs for subdivision and road earthworks in the Palmerston and Litchfield growth areas
  • PSIs and DSIs for industrial land deals at East Arm, Berrimah and Winnellie
  • Waste classification and PFAS screening for airport precinct and Defence-adjacent works
  • Quarry and extractive compliance in the rural area
  • Asbestos registers and sampling for Darwin and Palmerston properties
  • Flora and fauna surveys for clearing permits in the rural area
  • Weed hygiene declarations and inspections for rural area civil works

Services available
