Environmental consultant for the Top End

Remote Top End projects fail on logistics more than science. Bell Enviro has delivered compliance auditing and CEMP implementation for water and distribution network upgrades in Jabiru and is set up for remote mobilisation: self-sufficient field kit, sample cold-chain to Darwin laboratories, and the permits and inductions that Aboriginal land and Kakadu require. Fieldwork is planned around the Dry season, with authoring and reporting through the Wet.

Areas covered

Katherine and the Katherine region, Pine Creek and Adelaide River, Kakadu and Jabiru, West Arnhem including Maningrida and Gunbalanya, East Arnhem including Nhulunbuy and Galiwin'ku, the Tiwi Islands, Wadeye and the Daly River region, Timber Creek and the Victoria River district.

Typical work in this region

  • CEMP implementation and compliance auditing for remote community infrastructure upgrades
  • Contaminated land and fuel storage investigations at remote facilities and community sites
  • Weed hygiene and biosecurity planning for mobilising plant into Arnhem Land and the Tiwi Islands
  • AAPA and Traditional Owner engagement coordination for works on Aboriginal land
  • Environmental approvals support for extractive and infrastructure projects in the Katherine region

Services available

  • Contaminated land assessment — PSI and DSI
  • PFAS investigation and management
  • Construction Environmental Management Plans (CEMPs) and ESCPs
  • Environmental auditing, inspections and compliance
  • Environmental approvals and statutory compliance
  • Your environmental department, as a service
  • Cultural heritage assessment and AAPA clearance coordination
  • Compliance, stockpile and incident registers
