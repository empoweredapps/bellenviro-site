The rest of the NT and the Kimberley

For work outside the Top End, Bell Enviro is the Darwin-based alternative to flying a team in from Perth, Adelaide or Brisbane. Kununurra is a shorter drive from Darwin than it is a flight from Perth, and the country is the same: Wet season, savanna, Aboriginal land, long distances. WA jobs are done under the Contaminated Sites Act 2003 and the Environmental Protection Act 1986 with the DWER guidelines.

Areas covered

Tennant Creek and the Barkly, Alice Springs and Central Australia, the Gulf and Borroloola, and over the border to Kununurra, Wyndham, Lake Argyle, Halls Creek, Fitzroy Crossing and the East Kimberley.

Typical work in this region

  • Contaminated land investigations and waste classification at mining support, pastoral and infrastructure sites
  • CEMPs and site inspections for road, water and community infrastructure projects
  • PFAS screening at airstrips, depots and fire training sites
  • Environmental audits of remote operations for principals and financiers
  • Approvals scoping for NT and WA proponents

Services available

  • Contaminated land assessment: PSI and DSI
  • PFAS testing, investigation and management
  • Environmental management plans (CEMPs) and ESCPs
  • Environmental audits, inspections and compliance
  • Environmental approvals, NT EPA referrals and permits
  • Your environmental department, as a service
  • Cultural heritage and AAPA certificates
  • Compliance, stockpile and incident registers
  • Asbestos testing, registers and hazmat surveys
  • Soil testing in Darwin and the Top End
  • Bore water, surface water and discharge testing in Darwin
