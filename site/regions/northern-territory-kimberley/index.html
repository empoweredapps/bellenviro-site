Environmental consultant across the Northern Territory and the Kimberley

For projects outside the Top End, Bell Enviro offers a Darwin-based alternative to flying a team from Perth, Adelaide or Brisbane. For East Kimberley work the drive from Darwin is shorter than the flight from Perth, and the environmental and cultural context, Wet season, savanna, Aboriginal land and remote logistics, is the same one Bell Enviro works in every week. Work in Western Australia is delivered under WA's Contaminated Sites Act 2003 and Environmental Protection Act 1986 with the relevant DWER guidelines.

Areas covered

Tennant Creek and the Barkly, Alice Springs and Central Australia, the Gulf region and Borroloola, and across the border to Kununurra, Wyndham, Lake Argyle, Halls Creek, Fitzroy Crossing and the East Kimberley.

Typical work in this region

  • Contaminated land investigations and waste classification for mining support, pastoral and infrastructure sites
  • CEMPs and environmental inspections for road, water and community infrastructure projects
  • PFAS screening at airstrips, depots and fire training sites
  • Environmental audits of remote operations for principals and financiers
  • Approvals pathway scoping for NT and WA proponents

Services available

  • Contaminated land assessment — PSI and DSI
  • PFAS investigation and management
  • Construction Environmental Management Plans (CEMPs) and ESCPs
  • Environmental auditing, inspections and compliance
  • Environmental approvals and statutory compliance
  • Your environmental department, as a service
  • Cultural heritage assessment and AAPA clearance coordination
  • Compliance, stockpile and incident registers
