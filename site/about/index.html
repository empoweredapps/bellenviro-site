About Bell Enviro and Chris Bell

Bell Enviro is Chris Bell, an environmental scientist in Humpty Doo who works as the environmental capability inside civil contractors' and developers' teams across the NT.

How the practice works

Chris does the work or directly supervises it. That's deliberate. What you're paying an environmental consultant for is the judgement and the signature, so the person who quotes the job is the one on site, reading the data and signing the report. The report production and data handling are automated as far as they sensibly can be, which is why the prices are what they are and the turnaround is quick.

Experience

  • Environmental compliance auditing and CEMP implementation for water and distribution network upgrades in Jabiru, under an ongoing retainer with a civil head contractor
  • Waste classification and environmental assessment for apron expansion works in the Darwin International Airport precinct
  • Soil quality and contamination investigations for East Arm and Darwin industrial land transactions
  • Extractive and quarry environmental compliance in the Darwin rural area
  • Weed hygiene declarations, erosion and sediment control and incident reporting systems for live civil works
  • Asbestos and hazmat surveys, registers and Class B clearances
  • Flora and fauna surveys and wildlife management plans for clearing, Defence and civil works
  • Earlier work across Defence, aviation, oil and gas, mining, community development and cultural heritage in the NT and overseas

Credentials and cover

  • Environmental professional with over 25 years' experience, most of it in the Northern Territory
  • ISO 14001 environmental management systems auditor trained; audits to ISO 19011
  • Professional Indemnity and Public Liability insurance current
  • ICN Gateway registered
  • Working knowledge of Commonwealth, NT EPA, Defence, AAPA, ALRA, NLC and NTG frameworks

Partners

Bigger programs draw on a bench of field techs, drillers, archaeologists, anthropologists, ecologists and licensed asbestos assessors that Bell Enviro has worked with before, and on Darwin's NATA-accredited labs. Interstate consultancies also use Bell Enviro as their Darwin field capacity, working to their own SAQPs and templates.
