About Bell Enviro and Chris Bell

Bell Enviro is the practice of Chris Bell, an environmental scientist based in the Darwin rural area who works as the environmental capability inside civil contractors' and developers' project teams across the Northern Territory.

How the practice works

Every engagement is delivered or directly supervised by Chris. That is deliberate: the defensible value of an environmental consultant is the professional judgement and the signature on the report, so the person quoting the work is the person on site, interpreting the data and signing off. Report production, data handling and document assembly use modern tooling so that expert time is spent on the site, the data and the regulator conversation rather than formatting.

Experience

  • Environmental compliance auditing and CEMP implementation for water and distribution network upgrades in Jabiru, under an ongoing retainer with a civil head contractor
  • Waste classification and environmental assessment for apron expansion works in the Darwin International Airport precinct
  • Soil quality and contamination investigations for East Arm and Darwin industrial land transactions
  • Extractive and quarry environmental compliance in the Darwin rural area
  • Weed hygiene declarations, erosion and sediment control and incident reporting systems for live civil works
  • Earlier career across Defence, aviation, oil and gas, mining, community development and cultural heritage projects in the NT and internationally

Credentials and cover

  • Certified Environmental Practitioner (Site Contamination) application in progress with EIANZ
  • Professional Indemnity and Public Liability insurance current
  • ICN Gateway registered
  • Working knowledge of Commonwealth, NT EPA, Defence, AAPA, ALRA, NLC and NTG frameworks

Working with subcontractors and partners

For larger programs Bell Enviro draws on a bench of trusted field technicians, drillers, archaeologists, anthropologists and ecologists, and works with Darwin's NATA-accredited laboratories. Interstate consultancies also engage Bell Enviro for Darwin-based field capacity delivered to their own SAQPs and templates.
