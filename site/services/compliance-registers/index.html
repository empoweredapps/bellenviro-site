Compliance, stockpile and incident registers

Most compliance failures on civil sites are record-keeping failures: the control was in place, but nobody can prove it. Bell Enviro sets up simple, field-usable registers for approval obligations, stockpiles and incidents, trains your supervisors to use them, and keeps them honest through periodic review. A hosted version is being piloted with an NT head contractor.

What is included

  • Obligations register mapping every approval, licence and contract condition to an owner and evidence
  • Stockpile register with classification, source, volume and disposal pathway for each stockpile
  • Incident and non-conformance register with investigation and close-out tracking
  • Monthly compliance status reporting for principals and superintendents
  • Audit-ready evidence packs at project completion

Who it is for

Civil contractors delivering projects with environmental approval conditions, especially those handling fill, contaminated or PFAS-impacted material, or working under NT Government and Defence contracts.

Pricing

Set-up fee per project plus optional monthly maintenance. Register the interest for the hosted version via the contact page.

Available in Darwin, Palmerston & Rural · Top End · NT & Kimberley.

Frequently asked questions

Is this software?

Today it is a structured system delivered in tools your site team already uses, set up and reviewed by Bell Enviro. A hosted version is in pilot; contact Bell Enviro to register interest.

Why a stockpile register?

Regulators and principals increasingly ask where every cubic metre of material came from and went. A stockpile register answers that in minutes instead of a week of searching dockets.
