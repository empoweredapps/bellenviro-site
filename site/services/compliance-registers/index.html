Compliance, stockpile and incident registers

Most compliance failures on civil sites aren't about the controls. The control was there. Nobody can prove it. Bell Enviro sets up plain registers for approval obligations, stockpiles and incidents, trains the supervisors to keep them, and checks them periodically. A hosted version is being trialled with an NT head contractor.

What is included

  • Obligations register: every approval, licence and contract condition, with an owner and the evidence
  • Stockpile register: classification, source, volume and where each pile went
  • Incident and non-conformance register with investigation and close-out tracking
  • Monthly compliance status for the principal or superintendent
  • An audit-ready evidence pack at completion

Who it is for

Civil contractors on projects with approval conditions, particularly anyone handling fill, contaminated or PFAS-impacted material, or working under NTG and Defence contracts.

Pricing

Set-up fee per project, plus optional monthly maintenance. Register interest in the hosted version through the contact page.

Available in Darwin, Palmerston & Rural · Top End · NT & Kimberley.

Frequently asked questions

Is this software?

Right now it's a structured system in tools your site team already uses, set up and reviewed by Bell Enviro. A hosted version is in trial. Get in touch if you want to be part of it.

Why a stockpile register?

Because regulators and principals now ask where every cubic metre came from and went. A stockpile register answers that in minutes instead of a week of digging through dockets.
