Flora and fauna surveys

Clearing permits, DAs, NT EPA referrals and most Defence jobs want to know what lives on the site before the dozer goes in. Bell Enviro does the vegetation and habitat assessment, the threatened species checks and targeted fauna surveys, and writes it up in the form the approval needs. Where a job needs a specialist (bats, frogs, seasonal bird surveys) Bell Enviro brings in an ecologist it has worked with before and manages the program.

What is included

  • Vegetation mapping and habitat assessment for land clearing permits and DAs
  • Threatened species and sensitive vegetation checks (EPBC Act and NT TPWC Act listings)
  • Targeted fauna surveys: trapping, camera traps, call playback, spotlighting
  • Pre-clearing surveys and fauna spotter-catcher during clearing
  • Wildlife management plans for construction sites and Defence estate works
  • Weed and vegetation condition assessments for CEMPs and offsets

Who it is for

Developers and landowners applying for clearing permits, civil contractors with pre-clearing conditions, Defence contractors, and proponents with NT EPA or EPBC referrals.

Pricing

Fixed price for desktop assessments and single-visit surveys. Multi-season survey programs are scoped per project.

Available in Darwin, Palmerston & Rural · Top End · NT & Kimberley.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a flora and fauna survey for a clearing permit?

Usually yes, if the clearing is above the exempt area or near sensitive vegetation, waterways or threatened species habitat. The Land Clearing Guidelines expect a description of the vegetation and habitat, and the Development Consent Authority will ask for one if it isn't there.

When is the best time to survey?

Depends on what you're looking for. Most fauna is best surveyed in the late Dry or early Wet. A desktop assessment and vegetation survey can be done any time, and that's often enough to tell whether a seasonal survey is needed at all.

Do you do pre-clearing fauna spotting?

Yes. Pre-clearing inspections and a spotter-catcher on site during clearing, which is a condition on a lot of NTG and Defence contracts.
