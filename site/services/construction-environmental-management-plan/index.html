Construction Environmental Management Plans (CEMPs) and ESCPs

A CEMP is usually a condition of contract on NT Government, Defence, council and private civil works. Bell Enviro writes CEMPs that superintendents approve first time, because they are built from the actual contract conditions, site constraints and the contractor's real methodology, not a generic template.

What is included

  • Construction Environmental Management Plans to NTG, Defence and council specifications
  • Erosion and Sediment Control Plans (ESCPs) for the Top End Wet season
  • Weed hygiene and management plans, weed hygiene declarations and vehicle wash-down procedures
  • Dust, noise, vibration and water quality management plans
  • Incident and non-conformance procedures that contractors will actually use
  • CEMP implementation support: inductions, inspections and monitoring programs

Who it is for

Civil head contractors and subcontractors tendering for or delivering roads, water and sewer, subdivisions, airfield, Defence estate and remote community works across the NT.

Pricing

Fixed price per plan, scoped to the contract requirements. Implementation support at day rates or under a retainer.

Available in Darwin, Palmerston & Rural · Top End · NT & Kimberley.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a CEMP for NT Government civil works?

Most NT Government, Defence and council construction contracts require a CEMP before site establishment, and many require it to be approved by the superintendent. Check the contract's environmental clauses; Bell Enviro can review them with you.

How quickly can you turn a CEMP around?

A standard civil works CEMP is typically delivered within five to ten business days of receiving the contract documents and methodology. Urgent turnaround is available.

Can you also implement the CEMP on site?

Yes. Bell Enviro provides environmental inspections, monitoring, incident support and close-out reporting for live civil works, either per visit or under a retainer.
