Environmental management plans (CEMPs) and ESCPs

On most NT Government, Defence and council civil jobs the CEMP has to be approved before you can set up on site. A generic plan with the contract name swapped in gets sent back. Bell Enviro writes the plan from the actual contract clauses, the site and your methodology, so the superintendent approves it first time and your crew can actually follow it.

What is included

  • CEMPs to NTG, Defence and council specifications
  • Erosion and Sediment Control Plans that allow for a Top End Wet season
  • Weed hygiene and management: plans, declarations and wash-down procedures
  • Dust, noise, vibration and water quality management
  • Incident and non-conformance procedures that a site supervisor will actually use
  • Help running the plan: inductions, inspections and monitoring

Who it is for

Civil head contractors and subcontractors on roads, water and sewer, subdivisions, airfield, Defence estate and remote community work across the NT.

Pricing

Fixed price per plan, scoped to the contract. Site support at a day rate or under a retainer.

Available in Darwin, Palmerston & Rural · Top End · NT & Kimberley.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a CEMP for NT Government civil works?

Almost always. NTG, Defence and council construction contracts require one before site establishment, and most need the superintendent to approve it. Check the environmental clauses in your contract, or send them to Bell Enviro to check.

How quickly can you turn a CEMP around?

A standard civil works CEMP is normally five to ten business days from receiving the contract documents and your methodology. Faster is possible if the job is urgent.

Can you also implement the CEMP on site?

Yes. Inspections, monitoring, incident support and close-out reporting, either per visit or under a retainer.
