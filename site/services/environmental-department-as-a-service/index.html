Your environmental department, as a service

Most mid-sized civil contractors need an environmental person but not a full-time one. The retainer gives you Chris on call: tender reviews, CEMPs, incidents, regulator calls and site inspections, with one number to ring and your jobs at the front of the queue.

What is included

  • Monthly base fee covering availability, advice and document reviews
  • CEMPs, audits, investigations and approvals delivered at agreed rates, ahead of non-retainer work
  • Environmental clause review on tenders before you price them
  • Incident and regulator response, including first-call attendance
  • Inductions and toolbox material for your crews
  • Only a handful of contractors at a time, so response stays fast

Who it is for

NT civil contractors with steady NTG, Defence or council work who want the environmental capability of a bigger firm without the salary.

Pricing

Monthly retainer with set inclusions and agreed rates for deliverables. Three-month minimum.

Available in Darwin, Palmerston & Rural · Top End · NT & Kimberley.

Frequently asked questions

How is this different from hiring an environmental officer?

You get senior, insured, sign-off-capable expertise for less than a junior's salary, with no recruitment, leave or supervising. The trade-off is that Chris is shared with a small number of other clients.

What's in the base fee?

Availability, phone and email advice, document and tender reviews, and a set number of hours for inspections or meetings. Bigger deliverables are priced at rates agreed up front.

Can we start with one project?

Yes. Most retainer clients started with one CEMP or audit and moved across when the second job came in.
