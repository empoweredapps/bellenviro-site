Your environmental department, as a service

Mid-tier civil contractors need an environmental capability but rarely have enough work for a full-time environmental officer. The retainer gives you a principal-level environmental scientist on call: tender reviews, CEMPs, incident response, regulator contact and site inspections, with one number to call and priority turnaround.

What is included

  • Monthly base fee for standing availability, advice and document review
  • Priority delivery of CEMPs, audits, investigations and approvals at agreed drawdown rates
  • Tender and contract environmental clause review before you price the job
  • Incident and regulator response support, including first-call attendance
  • Environmental inductions and toolbox content for your crews
  • Limited to a small number of contractors so response times stay fast

Who it is for

NT civil contractors with regular NT Government, Defence or council work who want the compliance capability of a larger firm at a fraction of the cost of an employee.

Pricing

Monthly retainer with defined inclusions and agreed drawdown rates for deliverables. Three-month minimum.

Available in Darwin, Palmerston & Rural · Top End · NT & Kimberley.

Frequently asked questions

How is this different from hiring an environmental officer?

You get senior, insured, sign-off-capable expertise for less than a junior salary, with no recruitment, leave or supervision. The trade-off is that availability is shared across a small number of clients.

What is included in the base fee?

Standing availability, phone and email advice, document and tender review, and a set number of inspection or meeting hours. Larger deliverables are priced at drawdown rates agreed up front.

Can we start with a single project?

Yes. Many clients start with one CEMP or audit and move to the retainer once the second project arrives.
