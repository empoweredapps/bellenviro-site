Asbestos testing, registers and hazardous materials surveys

Most buildings in Darwin built before 1990, and much of the post-Cyclone Tracy rebuild, contain asbestos. Before you renovate, demolish, excavate near old building slabs or buy a commercial property, you need to know what is there. Bell Enviro provides asbestos identification sampling with NATA-accredited laboratory analysis, asbestos registers and management plans that satisfy NT Work Health and Safety requirements, and pre-demolition hazardous materials surveys. Non-friable (Class B) works are handled directly; for friable asbestos and Class A removals Bell Enviro brings in a licensed asbestos assessor or occupational hygienist and manages the job end to end.

What is included

  • Asbestos identification: sampling of suspect materials with NATA-accredited laboratory analysis and written results
  • Asbestos registers and asbestos management plans for workplaces, as required under the NT Work Health and Safety (National Uniform Legislation) Regulations
  • Pre-demolition and pre-refurbishment hazardous materials surveys: asbestos, lead paint, synthetic mineral fibre, PCBs, ozone-depleting substances
  • Asbestos in soil: assessment, sampling and waste classification for civil works and contaminated land investigations
  • Clearance inspections and certificates for non-friable (Class B) removal works
  • Coordination of licensed asbestos assessors and occupational hygienists for friable (Class A) removals, air monitoring and clearance
  • Removal contractor scoping, specification and supervision

Who it is for

Home renovators and property buyers in Darwin and Palmerston, builders and demolition contractors, body corporates and commercial property managers needing a register, and civil contractors who have struck asbestos fragments in fill.

Pricing

Fixed price for identification sampling (per sample, laboratory included) and for registers and surveys scoped by building size. Clearance inspections at a fixed rate per inspection.

Available in Darwin, Palmerston & Rural · Top End · NT & Kimberley.

Frequently asked questions

How much does asbestos testing cost in Darwin?

Identification of a single suspect material, including NATA laboratory analysis and a written result, is a fixed per-sample price; a typical house inspection with several samples is quoted as a package. Contact Bell Enviro for current rates.

How long do asbestos results take?

Laboratory identification is usually two to three business days from receipt, with same-day or next-day options available at additional cost for urgent demolition or incident situations.

Do I legally need an asbestos register?

Yes for any workplace building constructed before 31 December 2003 where asbestos is or may be present. For homes a register is not mandatory but is strongly recommended before any renovation and is often required by builders before they will quote.

Who can do a clearance inspection in the NT?

Non-friable (Class B) removal clearances can be done by a competent person independent of the removalist. Friable (Class A) removals require a licensed asbestos assessor. Bell Enviro does the former directly and engages a licensed assessor for the latter.

What if asbestos is found in soil on a civil site?

Stop work in that area and get it assessed. Bell Enviro samples and classifies the material, advises on management or disposal under the ASC NEPM and NT EPA requirements, and documents the clearance so works can resume.
