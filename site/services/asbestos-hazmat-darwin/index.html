Asbestos testing, registers and hazmat surveys

Most Darwin buildings from before 1990, and a lot of the post-Tracy rebuild, have asbestos in them. Before you renovate, demolish, dig near an old slab or buy a commercial building, find out what's there. Bell Enviro samples suspect materials and sends them to a NATA-accredited lab, writes asbestos registers and management plans that meet NT WHS requirements, and does pre-demolition hazmat surveys. Chris has substantial hazmat experience and handles non-friable (Class B) work directly. For friable asbestos and Class A removals Bell Enviro brings in a licensed asbestos assessor or occupational hygienist and manages the job end to end.

What is included

  • Asbestos identification: sampling of suspect materials, NATA-accredited lab analysis, written result
  • Asbestos registers and management plans for workplaces under the NT WHS Regulations
  • Pre-demolition and pre-refurbishment hazmat surveys: asbestos, lead paint, synthetic mineral fibre, PCBs, ozone-depleting substances
  • Asbestos in soil: assessment, sampling and waste classification on civil sites
  • Clearance inspections and certificates for non-friable (Class B) removals
  • Licensed assessors and hygienists arranged for friable (Class A) removals, air monitoring and clearance
  • Scoping, specifying and supervising removal contractors

Who it is for

Renovators and property buyers in Darwin and Palmerston, builders and demolition contractors, body corporates and commercial property managers who need a register, and civil crews who have found asbestos fragments in fill.

Pricing

Fixed per-sample price for identification, lab included. Registers and surveys are quoted on building size. Clearance inspections at a fixed rate per visit.

Available in Darwin, Palmerston & Rural · Top End · NT & Kimberley.

Frequently asked questions

How much does asbestos testing cost in Darwin?

A single suspect material, with NATA lab analysis and a written result, is a fixed per-sample price. A typical house with a handful of samples is quoted as a package. Ask Bell Enviro for current rates.

How long do asbestos results take?

Two to three business days from the lab receiving the samples. Same-day or next-day is available at extra cost if demolition is waiting or there's been an incident.

Do I legally need an asbestos register?

Yes, for any workplace building built before 31 December 2003 where asbestos is or might be present. For a house it's not compulsory, but get one before renovating. A lot of builders won't quote without it.

Who can do a clearance inspection in the NT?

For non-friable (Class B) removals, a competent person independent of the removalist. For friable (Class A), a licensed asbestos assessor. Bell Enviro does the first directly and brings in a licensed assessor for the second.

We've found asbestos in soil on site. Now what?

Stop work in that area and get it assessed. Bell Enviro samples and classifies the material, advises on management or disposal under the ASC NEPM and NT EPA requirements, and documents the clearance so work can restart.
