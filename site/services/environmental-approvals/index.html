Environmental approvals, NT EPA referrals and permits

The NT has its own Environment Protection Act, Water Act, Planning Act and Weeds Management Act, plus the Commonwealth EPBC Act on top. Working out which ones apply to a project, and in what order, is half the job. Bell Enviro scopes the approvals a project actually needs, prepares the applications and deals with the agency.

What is included

  • Approvals scoping: which Acts, which agency, how long
  • NT EPA referrals and environmental impact assessment support
  • Environment protection licences for extractives, waste and industrial sites
  • Land clearing permits, water extraction and discharge licences
  • Environmental reports for development applications, and conditions compliance afterwards
  • EPBC Act self-assessment and referrals

Who it is for

Developers, quarry and extractive operators, civil contractors who have taken on the approvals obligations, and interstate proponents who don't know the NT system.

Pricing

Fixed price for scoping and standard applications. Larger assessments are scoped per project.

Available in Darwin, Palmerston & Rural · Top End · NT & Kimberley.

Frequently asked questions

Does my project need an NT EPA referral?

Only if it could have a significant impact on the environment. Most civil jobs don't. Extractives, large clearing and work in sensitive areas might. A short scoping review answers it early, before it becomes a program problem.

How long do NT environmental approvals take?

Simple licences and permits take weeks. An EPA assessment takes many months. The biggest time saver is starting the scoping early.

Can you deal with the agency for me?

Yes. Bell Enviro deals with the NT EPA, DEPWS, the Development Consent Authority and AAPA on clients' behalf all the time.
