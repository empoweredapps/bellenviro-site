Environmental approvals, NT EPA referrals and permits

Navigating the NT Environment Protection Act 2019, the Water Act, Planning Act, Weeds Management Act and Commonwealth EPBC Act takes someone who has dealt with the regulators before. Bell Enviro scopes which approvals a project actually needs, prepares the applications and supporting assessments, and manages the conversation with the agency.

What is included

  • Approvals pathway scoping: which Acts, which agency, how long
  • NT EPA referrals and environmental impact assessment support
  • Environment protection licences and approvals for extractives, waste and industrial activities
  • Land clearing, water extraction and waste discharge licence applications
  • Development application environmental reports and conditions compliance
  • EPBC Act self-assessment and referral support

Who it is for

Developers, quarry and extractive operators, civil contractors with self-performed approvals obligations, and interstate proponents unfamiliar with NT processes.

Pricing

Fixed price for pathway scoping and standard applications; scoped per project for assessments.

Available in Darwin, Palmerston & Rural · Top End · NT & Kimberley.

Frequently asked questions

Does my project need NT EPA referral?

Referral is required where a project has the potential to have a significant impact on the environment. Many civil projects do not, but extractives, large clearing and works in sensitive areas may. A short scoping review answers this early.

How long do NT environmental approvals take?

Simple licences and permits can take weeks; EPA assessments take many months. Starting the scoping early is the single biggest time saver.

Can you deal with the agency on my behalf?

Yes. Bell Enviro routinely liaises with NT EPA, DEPWS, Development Consent Authority and AAPA on behalf of clients.
