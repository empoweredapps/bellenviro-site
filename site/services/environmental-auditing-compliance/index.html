Environmental audits, inspections and compliance

An audit should tell you what is wrong, who fixes it and by when. Bell Enviro audits against your approvals, licences, CEMP and contract conditions, following ISO 19011, and writes the findings as a short action list rather than a long report nobody reads.

What is included

  • Compliance audits against approvals, licences, CEMPs and contract conditions
  • Regular site inspections on live civil works
  • Monitoring programs: water, dust, noise, stockpiles, sediment controls
  • Incident and non-conformance systems your own people can run
  • Retrospective compliance reviews when the records have gaps
  • Close-out and completion reports at handover

Who it is for

Head contractors who want an independent look before the principal's auditor arrives. Principals and project managers who want to know how the contractor is going. Operators holding NT EPA licences or approvals.

Pricing

Fixed price per audit or inspection program, or a day rate.

Available in Darwin, Palmerston & Rural · Top End · NT & Kimberley.

Frequently asked questions

What does an environmental audit involve?

A read of your approvals and plans, a site walk, a chat with the site staff, and a report listing what conforms, what doesn't, and what to do about it.

How often should a civil site be inspected?

Weekly during earthworks and through the Wet. Fortnightly or monthly once the site has stabilised. A lot of contracts set the frequency.

Our records have gaps. Can that be fixed?

Bell Enviro can rebuild monitoring, inspection and incident records from whatever evidence exists, say plainly where the gaps are, and set up a system so it doesn't happen again.
