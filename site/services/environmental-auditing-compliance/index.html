Environmental auditing, inspections and compliance

Independent environmental audits and compliance inspections that give head contractors, principals and financiers evidence that conditions are being met. Audits follow ISO 19011 principles, and findings are written as actionable items with owners and dates, not pages of generic observations.

What is included

  • Environmental compliance audits against approvals, licences, CEMPs and contract conditions
  • Scheduled site environmental inspections for live civil works
  • Monitoring programs: water quality, dust, noise, stockpiles and sediment controls
  • Incident reporting and non-conformance systems contractors can run themselves
  • Retrospective compliance reviews where records have gaps
  • Close-out and completion reporting for project handover

Who it is for

Head contractors needing an independent check before a principal's audit, principals and project managers wanting assurance on contractor performance, and operators holding NT EPA environment protection licences or approvals.

Pricing

Fixed price per audit or inspection program, or day rate.

Available in Darwin, Palmerston & Rural · Top End · NT & Kimberley.

Frequently asked questions

What does an environmental audit involve?

A document review of your approvals and plans, a site inspection, interviews with site staff, and a report of conformances, non-conformances and opportunities for improvement, each with a recommended action.

How often should civil sites be inspected?

Weekly inspections are typical during earthworks and the Wet season, extending to fortnightly or monthly once the site is stabilised. Many contracts specify a frequency.

Can you fix a project's compliance records retrospectively?

Bell Enviro can reconstruct monitoring, inspection and incident records from available evidence, identify gaps honestly, and set up a system so the gaps do not recur.
