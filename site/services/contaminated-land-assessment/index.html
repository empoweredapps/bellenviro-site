Contaminated land assessment: PSI and DSI

Buying a block, lodging a DA, pulling out an old fuel tank, or hit something that smells wrong in a trench. These are the jobs that need a site investigation, and sometimes an independent audit on top. Bell Enviro does them to the ASC NEPM and NT EPA guidelines, with NATA-accredited labs, and writes the report in a way that the regulator, the auditor and the bank can all use.

What is included

  • Preliminary Site Investigation (PSI): site history, records, walkover, conceptual site model
  • Detailed Site Investigation (DSI): soil, groundwater and vapour sampling, designed to answer the actual question
  • Waste classification for fill and excavated material (VENM, ENM, contaminated fill)
  • Fuel tank decommissioning: validation sampling and the paperwork to close it out
  • Acid sulfate soil assessment and management plans
  • Validation and clearance reports for auditors and the NT EPA
  • Contaminated land audits (site audit statements and reports) through a standing arrangement with an accredited contaminated land auditor

Who it is for

People buying or developing land in Darwin and Palmerston, civil contractors who have found something unexpected mid-job, Defence and airport precinct works, and interstate consultancies that need someone on the ground in Darwin.

Pricing

PSIs are a fixed price. DSIs are a fixed price for the design, fieldwork and report, with lab fees at cost. The scope is agreed before anything starts.

Available in Darwin, Palmerston & Rural · Top End · NT & Kimberley.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a PSI or a DSI?

Start with a PSI. It works out whether contamination is likely from the site's history and a walkover. If it finds a reason for concern, a DSI goes out and samples to confirm it and map how far it goes. Most land deals and DAs only ever need the PSI.

Can Bell Enviro arrange a contaminated land audit?

Yes. Bell Enviro has a formal arrangement with an accredited contaminated land auditor, so where a planning condition, the NT EPA or a purchaser requires an independent site audit, Bell Enviro does the investigation and manages the audit through to the audit statement, with one point of contact.

Will the NT EPA accept the report?

Reports are written to the ASC NEPM and NT EPA contaminated land guidelines. That is what the EPA and the contaminated land auditors assess against.

How long does a PSI take in Darwin?

Usually two to three weeks, mostly waiting on title searches and records. A DSI depends on lab turnaround, which is five to ten business days after sampling.
