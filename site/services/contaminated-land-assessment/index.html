Contaminated land assessment — PSI and DSI

Preliminary and detailed site investigations for land transactions, development approvals, fuel storage decommissioning and civil works. Delivered in line with the National Environment Protection (Assessment of Site Contamination) Measure 1999 (ASC NEPM) and NT EPA guidance, with NATA-accredited laboratory analysis.

What is included

  • Preliminary Site Investigation (PSI): site history, desktop review, walkover and conceptual site model
  • Detailed Site Investigation (DSI): soil, groundwater and vapour sampling design and execution
  • Waste classification and off-site disposal documentation (VENM/ENM and contaminated fill)
  • Underground and above-ground fuel storage decommissioning and validation
  • Acid sulfate soil assessment and management plans
  • Site validation and clearance reporting for auditors and regulators

Who it is for

Developers and land purchasers doing due diligence, civil head contractors encountering unexpected fill or odours mid-project, Defence and aviation precinct works, and interstate consultancies needing Darwin-based field capacity.

Pricing

PSIs are fixed price. DSIs are fixed price for design, fieldwork and reporting with laboratory analysis at cost.

Available in Darwin, Palmerston & Rural · Top End · NT & Kimberley.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a PSI or a DSI?

A PSI is the first step: it establishes whether contamination is plausible from the site's history and a walkover. If the PSI identifies potential contamination, a DSI collects samples to confirm and delineate it. Most land transactions and development applications start with a PSI.

Will the NT EPA accept the report?

Reports are prepared to the ASC NEPM and NT EPA contaminated land guidelines, which is the standard NT regulators and contaminated land auditors assess against.

How long does a PSI take in Darwin?

Typically two to three weeks from engagement, depending on title search and records turnaround. DSIs depend on laboratory turnaround, usually five to ten business days after sampling.
