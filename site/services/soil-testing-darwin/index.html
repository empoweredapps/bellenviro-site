Soil testing in Darwin and the Top End

Soil testing only helps if the right things are tested for, the samples are taken properly and someone explains what the numbers mean. Bell Enviro designs the sampling, collects it, uses NATA-accredited labs in Darwin and interstate, and tells you in plain terms what the results mean for your block, your disposal docket or your DA.

What is included

  • Contamination screening for a property purchase, DA or rezoning
  • Waste classification of fill, excavated material and stockpiles for disposal or reuse
  • PFAS soil testing to NEMP protocols
  • Acid sulfate soil testing (field pH and lab chromium-reducible sulfur)
  • Asbestos in soil screening and quantification
  • Hydrocarbons, metals, pesticides and nutrients, interpreted against ASC NEPM and NT EPA criteria
  • Agricultural and horticultural soil testing on request

Who it is for

Landowners and buyers in Darwin, Palmerston and the Litchfield rural area, builders and civil contractors classifying material, and project managers dealing with an unexpected find.

Pricing

Fixed price per sampling visit plus lab fees at cost. The analysis suite is agreed before sampling so there are no surprises on the invoice.

Frequently asked questions

How much does soil testing cost in Darwin?

A basic contamination screen on one sample for metals and hydrocarbons is in the low hundreds including the lab. Classifying a stockpile depends on its volume, because the NEPM and NT EPA set a minimum number of samples per cubic metre. Bell Enviro quotes the whole program up front.

Can I collect the samples myself?

You can, but labs and regulators only rely on results where the method, containers and chain of custody are documented. Badly collected samples are the most common reason results get thrown out, and a sampling visit is usually cheaper than doing it twice.

Which lab do you use?

NATA-accredited labs with Darwin receipt for standard suites, and specialist interstate labs for PFAS and asbestos quantification. The lab certificate comes attached to the report.
