Soil testing in Darwin and the Top End

Soil testing is only useful if the right analytes are chosen, the samples are collected properly and someone interprets the numbers against the correct criteria. Bell Enviro designs the sampling, collects it, uses NATA-accredited laboratories in Darwin and interstate, and tells you what the results mean for your site, your waste disposal docket or your development application.

What is included

  • Contamination screening for property purchase, development applications and rezoning
  • Waste classification of fill, excavated material and stockpiles for off-site disposal or reuse
  • PFAS soil testing to PFAS NEMP protocols
  • Acid sulfate soil testing (field pH and laboratory chromium-reducible sulfur)
  • Asbestos in soil screening and quantification
  • Hydrocarbon, heavy metal, pesticide and nutrient analysis with interpretation against ASC NEPM and NT EPA criteria
  • Agricultural and horticultural soil testing arranged on request

Who it is for

Landowners and buyers in Darwin, Palmerston and the Litchfield rural area, builders and civil contractors needing to classify material, and project managers responding to unexpected finds on site.

Pricing

Fixed price per sampling visit plus laboratory analysis at cost, with the analysis suite agreed before sampling so there are no surprises.

Frequently asked questions

How much does soil testing cost in Darwin?

A basic contamination screen of one sample for metals and hydrocarbons is in the low hundreds of dollars including laboratory fees; a waste classification program for a stockpile depends on volume, as NEPM and NT EPA require a minimum number of samples per cubic metre. Bell Enviro quotes the full program up front.

Can I collect the samples myself?

You can, but laboratories and regulators will only rely on results where the sampling method, containers and chain of custody are documented, and incorrectly collected samples are the most common reason results get rejected. Bell Enviro's sampling visit is usually cheaper than re-doing it.

Which laboratory do you use?

NATA-accredited laboratories with Darwin receipt facilities for standard suites, and specialist interstate laboratories for PFAS and asbestos quantification. Results are reported with the laboratory certificate attached.
