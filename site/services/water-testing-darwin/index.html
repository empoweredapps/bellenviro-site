Bore water, surface water and discharge testing in Darwin

Rural area houses run on bores. Civil sites pump out groundwater. Approvals want the creek monitored. Bell Enviro samples bores, monitoring wells and surface water properly, with purging, field parameters and chain of custody, and reports the results against the criteria that apply: the Australian Drinking Water Guidelines for a household, ANZG guidelines and licence limits for a discharge.

What is included

  • Bore water testing for drinking, stock and irrigation use in the Darwin rural area
  • Groundwater monitoring well sampling for contaminated land and PFAS investigations
  • Dewatering and construction discharge sampling against NT EPA and licence criteria
  • Surface water and sediment monitoring for CEMPs and approvals
  • Field parameters: pH, conductivity, dissolved oxygen, turbidity
  • Plain-English results

Who it is for

Rural area homeowners and buyers on bore water, civil contractors with dewatering or discharge conditions, and developers with water monitoring obligations.

Pricing

Fixed price per sampling visit plus lab fees at cost. Monitoring programs priced per round.

Available in Darwin, Palmerston & Rural · Top End · NT & Kimberley.

Frequently asked questions

How much is bore water testing in Darwin?

A standard household suite (bacteria, metals, major ions, nitrate) including sampling and lab is a fixed package. Ask for the current price. Results are reported against the Australian Drinking Water Guidelines.

How often should I test my bore?

Bacteria at least yearly, and after flooding, works near the bore, or any change in taste or colour. A full chemistry suite every few years or when buying the property.

Can you test dewatering water before we discharge?

Yes. Bell Enviro samples for the parameters in your discharge condition with fast turnaround, and can set up the ongoing monitoring the condition requires.
