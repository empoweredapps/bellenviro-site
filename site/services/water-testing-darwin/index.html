Bore water, surface water and discharge testing in Darwin

Rural area households rely on bores, civil sites discharge dewatering water, and approvals require monitoring of creeks and drains. Bell Enviro samples bores, surface water and groundwater wells using proper purging, field parameters and chain of custody, and reports the results against the criteria that matter: Australian Drinking Water Guidelines for households, ANZG water quality guidelines and licence limits for discharges.

What is included

  • Bore water testing for drinking, stock and irrigation suitability in the Darwin rural area
  • Groundwater monitoring well sampling for contaminated land and PFAS investigations
  • Dewatering and construction discharge sampling against NT EPA and licence criteria
  • Surface water and sediment monitoring for CEMPs and approvals
  • Field parameters: pH, electrical conductivity, dissolved oxygen, turbidity
  • Interpretation and plain-English reporting

Who it is for

Rural area homeowners and buyers on bore water, civil contractors with dewatering or discharge conditions, and developers with water quality monitoring obligations.

Pricing

Fixed price per sampling visit plus laboratory analysis at cost. Monitoring programs priced per round.

Available in Darwin, Palmerston & Rural · Top End · NT & Kimberley.

Frequently asked questions

How much is bore water testing in Darwin?

A standard household potability suite (microbiology, metals, major ions, nitrate) including sampling and laboratory analysis is a fixed package; contact Bell Enviro for the current price. Results are reported against the Australian Drinking Water Guidelines.

How often should I test my bore?

Annually for microbiology at minimum, and after any flooding, works near the bore or change in taste or colour. A full chemistry suite every few years or when buying a property.

Can you test dewatering water before we discharge it?

Yes. Bell Enviro samples and gets rapid turnaround on the parameters in your discharge condition, and can set up the ongoing monitoring program the condition requires.
