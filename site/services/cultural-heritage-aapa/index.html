Cultural heritage assessment and AAPA clearance coordination

Sacred sites and heritage obligations apply to almost every ground-disturbing project in the Northern Territory. Bell Enviro coordinates AAPA Authority Certificates, heritage assessments and Traditional Owner engagement so that works are cleared before the excavator arrives, not after a stop-work notice.

What is included

  • AAPA Authority Certificate applications and clearance survey coordination
  • Heritage due diligence under the NT Heritage Act 2011
  • Traditional Owner and land council engagement and consultation records
  • Archaeological survey coordination with qualified archaeologists
  • Cultural heritage management plans and unexpected finds procedures for CEMPs

Who it is for

Civil contractors and developers working on Crown land, Aboriginal land, remote community projects and greenfield sites anywhere in the NT.

Pricing

Scoped per project; AAPA and specialist fees at cost.

Available in Darwin, Palmerston & Rural · Top End · NT & Kimberley.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need an AAPA certificate?

An Authority Certificate is not mandatory but provides a legal defence against prosecution for damaging a sacred site. Most NT Government and Defence contracts require one before ground disturbance.

How long does an AAPA certificate take?

Allow several months. Applications are lodged early in project planning, which is why Bell Enviro raises heritage in the first conversation about any project.

Do you run the consultation yourselves?

Bell Enviro coordinates consultation through AAPA and land councils and engages qualified anthropologists and archaeologists where the project requires specialist surveys.
