PFAS testing, investigation and management

PFAS is the firefighting foam chemical. Around Darwin it sits in the soil and groundwater near the airport, the RAAF base, old fire training grounds and some legacy industrial land, and it has moved along the drains from there. It also turns up where people don't expect it: old fire stations, and public spaces where foam was used for events and displays. If you are digging in those areas the material may need testing before it leaves site. Bell Enviro runs PFAS sampling to the PFAS National Environmental Management Plan with the right PFAS-free kit and NATA-accredited labs.

What is included

  • PFAS screening added to a PSI or DSI
  • Soil, groundwater, surface water and sediment sampling to NEMP protocols
  • Waste classification and a disposal or reuse pathway for excavated material
  • PFAS management plans for civil works in affected precincts
  • Advice on dewatering and stockpiles when PFAS-impacted water or soil turns up

Who it is for

Civil contractors working near Darwin International Airport, RAAF Base Darwin, East Arm and other PFAS areas. Developers buying old industrial land. Project managers who need to know where the dirt can go.

Pricing

Fixed price for the investigation design, fieldwork and report. Lab fees at cost.

Frequently asked questions

Where is PFAS a problem around Darwin?

The airport and RAAF base precincts and the suburbs and drains downstream of them, plus any site where firefighting foam was used or stored. In Chris's experience that includes old fire stations and, occasionally, parks and ovals where foam was sprayed for events. If you are excavating in those areas, assume you need to test before material leaves site.

Can PFAS-impacted soil be reused on site?

Often, yes, under a management plan consistent with the PFAS NEMP. Off-site disposal needs classification and a licensed facility, and there are not many of those in the NT, so on-site reuse is usually the practical answer.

How much does PFAS testing cost in Darwin?

More than a standard contamination suite. The samples go to specialist labs interstate, so a single soil or water sample is a few hundred dollars including the sampling. Bell Enviro quotes the whole program with the sample numbers the NEMP requires for your purpose.

What sampling precautions are needed?

PFAS is in waterproof clothing, sunscreen, some sample containers and a lot of normal field gear. If the sampling is not done with PFAS-free equipment and proper decontamination, the results are meaningless. Bell Enviro's protocols follow the NEMP.
