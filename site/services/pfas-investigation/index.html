PFAS testing, investigation and management

Per- and polyfluoroalkyl substances (PFAS) are a live issue around Darwin's airport, Defence and firefighting training precincts and legacy industrial land. Bell Enviro designs and runs PFAS investigations to the PFAS National Environmental Management Plan (NEMP), with contamination-free sampling protocols and NATA-accredited analysis.

What is included

  • PFAS screening within PSI/DSI programs
  • Soil, groundwater, surface water and sediment sampling to NEMP protocols
  • PFAS waste classification and disposal pathway advice for excavated material
  • PFAS management plans for civil works in affected precincts
  • Dewatering and stockpile management advice where PFAS-impacted water or soil is encountered

Who it is for

Civil contractors working in or near Darwin International Airport, RAAF Base Darwin, East Arm and other PFAS-affected precincts; developers acquiring legacy industrial land; project managers needing a disposal pathway for excavated material.

Pricing

Fixed price for investigation design, fieldwork and reporting; laboratory analysis at cost.

Available in Darwin, Palmerston & Rural · Top End · NT & Kimberley.

Frequently asked questions

Where is PFAS a concern in the Darwin region?

PFAS investigation areas around Darwin include the airport and RAAF Base precincts and surrounding suburbs, plus other sites where firefighting foam was historically used. Any excavation in these areas may need PFAS screening before material leaves site.

Can PFAS-impacted soil be reused on site?

Often yes, under a management plan consistent with the PFAS NEMP. Off-site disposal requires classification and an approved receiving facility, which is limited in the NT, so on-site reuse is usually the practical option.

How much does PFAS testing cost in Darwin?

Laboratory analysis for PFAS is more expensive than standard contaminant suites and samples go to specialist interstate laboratories, so a single soil or water PFAS sample is typically a few hundred dollars including sampling. Bell Enviro quotes the full program with the number of samples the NEMP requires for your purpose.

What sampling precautions are needed?

PFAS is present in many everyday materials, so sampling uses PFAS-free equipment, clothing and containers and strict decontamination. Bell Enviro's field protocols follow NEMP guidance.
