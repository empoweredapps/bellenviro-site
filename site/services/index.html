Environmental consulting services

Every service is scoped and priced before work starts and delivered by the principal. Pick the one that matches the contract clause or the problem on site.

Contaminated land assessment — PSI and DSI

NEPM-aligned preliminary and detailed site investigations (PSI/DSI) in Darwin, Palmerston and across the NT. Fixed-price, principal-delivered reports accepted by NT EPA and auditors.

PFAS testing, investigation and management

PFAS soil, groundwater and surface water investigations in Darwin and the NT, delivered to the PFAS National Environmental Management Plan. Sampling, waste classification and management plans for civil works.

Environmental management plans (CEMPs) and ESCPs

Contract-ready CEMPs, erosion and sediment control plans, weed hygiene and dust/noise plans for NT Government, Defence and council civil works. Fixed price, fast turnaround, Darwin-based.

Environmental auditing, inspections and compliance

ISO 19011-based environmental audits, site compliance inspections, monitoring programs and incident reporting systems for civil works in Darwin and across the NT.

Environmental approvals, NT EPA referrals and permits

NT EPA referrals, environment protection licences, land clearing and water approvals, and statutory environmental compliance advice for projects in Darwin, the Top End and the NT.

Your environmental department, as a service

A monthly retainer giving NT civil contractors standing environmental advice, document review, incident support and priority delivery of CEMPs, audits and investigations, without hiring an environmental officer.

Cultural heritage assessment and AAPA clearance coordination

Cultural heritage assessment, Aboriginal Areas Protection Authority (AAPA) certificate coordination, Traditional Owner engagement and archaeological survey support for projects in the NT.

Compliance, stockpile and incident registers

Practical obligations, stockpile and incident register systems for mid-tier civil contractors, set up and maintained by Bell Enviro so compliance evidence is ready when the auditor or principal asks.

Asbestos testing, registers and hazardous materials surveys

Asbestos sampling and NATA-accredited identification, asbestos registers and management plans, pre-demolition hazmat surveys and Class B clearance inspections in Darwin, Palmerston and the NT. Licensed assessors engaged for friable and Class A works.

Soil testing in Darwin and the Top End

Soil testing and sampling in Darwin, Palmerston and the rural area: contamination screening, waste classification for fill and excavated material, PFAS, acid sulfate soils and asbestos in soil. NATA-accredited laboratories, interpreted results.

Bore water, surface water and discharge testing in Darwin

Bore water, surface water, groundwater, dewatering and discharge sampling in Darwin, Palmerston and the rural area, with NATA-accredited analysis and interpreted results against drinking water, irrigation and NT EPA criteria.
