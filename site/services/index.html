Environmental consulting services

Everything is scoped and priced before it starts, and Chris does the work. Pick the one that matches the contract clause or the problem on site.

Asbestos testing, registers and hazmat surveys

Asbestos sampling with NATA-accredited identification, asbestos registers and management plans, pre-demolition hazmat surveys and Class B clearance inspections in Darwin, Palmerston and the NT. Licensed assessors brought in for friable and Class A work.

Soil testing in Darwin and the Top End

Soil sampling and testing in Darwin, Palmerston and the rural area: contamination screening, waste classification of fill and excavated material, PFAS, acid sulfate soils and asbestos in soil. NATA labs, results explained.

PFAS testing, investigation and management

PFAS testing of soil, groundwater and surface water in Darwin and the NT, done to the PFAS NEMP. Sampling, waste classification and management plans for civil works near the airport, RAAF base and East Arm.

Bore water, surface water and discharge testing in Darwin

Bore water, groundwater, surface water, dewatering and discharge sampling in Darwin, Palmerston and the rural area, with NATA-accredited analysis and results explained against drinking water, irrigation and NT EPA criteria.

Environmental approvals, NT EPA referrals and permits

NT EPA referrals, environment protection licences, land clearing and water permits, and approvals advice for projects in Darwin, the Top End and the NT.

Sacred site clearances (AAPA certificates) and cultural heritage

AAPA Authority Certificate applications, heritage due diligence, Traditional Owner engagement and archaeological survey coordination for projects anywhere in the NT.

Environmental management plans (CEMPs) and ESCPs

Contract-ready CEMPs, erosion and sediment control plans, weed hygiene and dust and noise plans for NT Government, Defence and council civil works. Fixed price, usually within five to ten business days.

Contaminated land assessment: PSI and DSI

Preliminary and detailed site investigations (PSI/DSI) in Darwin, Palmerston and across the NT. Done to the ASC NEPM, fixed price, written and signed by the person who did the work.

Flora and fauna surveys

Flora and fauna (wildlife) surveys, threatened species checks and vegetation assessments for land clearing permits, development applications, Defence works and CEMPs in Darwin, the Top End and the NT.

Environmental audits, inspections and compliance

Independent environmental audits, site inspections, monitoring programs and incident systems for civil works in Darwin and across the NT. Findings written as actions, not pages of observations.

Your environmental department, as a service

A monthly retainer that gives NT civil contractors an environmental scientist on call for advice, document reviews, incident response and priority delivery of CEMPs, audits and investigations, without hiring one.

Compliance, stockpile and incident registers

Simple obligations, stockpile and incident registers for civil contractors, set up and kept honest by Bell Enviro so the evidence is there when the auditor or the principal asks.
